  2. THE GOLDEN RULE (READ BEFORE DEMOING)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This is a mockup, not a live application. Every intended interaction shows a
toast confirmation, opens a real dropdown, expands a real drawer, or updates
real on-page state. If you click something and NOTHING happens, that element
is decorative — don't click it in front of the audience again. The lists
below tell you exactly which are which.

DO click any element listed under "CLICKABLE".
DO NOT click any element listed under "DO NOT CLICK" during the demo — they
look interactive but are display-only, and dead clicks break the illusion.

  4. FIVE-MINUTE DEMO SCRIPT (with in-line click cues)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Below, every action starts with an instruction in [SQUARE BRACKETS]. Anything
NOT in brackets is your spoken talk-track. Stay off elements listed in the
"DO NOT CLICK" sections above between cues — silence is fine.

────────────────────────────────────────
  OPENING (30 seconds) — no clicks yet
────────────────────────────────────────
"This is what Dynamics 365 Business Central looks like once we've woven
REACH's agents into it. Same familiar D365 chrome your finance team already
knows — but every tile is now an intelligent agent working alongside them.
Reach on the top left. Cognizant on the top right. Everything in between is
what a Finance Manager sees at 09:00 on a Monday morning."


────────────────────────────────────────
  ACT 1 — AGENTS ARE ALWAYS ON (60 seconds)
────────────────────────────────────────

[GESTURE at the hero headline "$3.4M of finance activity"]
"By the time our controller logged in this morning, agents had already
processed three-and-a-half million pounds of finance activity overnight.
Zero unreviewed exceptions. Eight agents online."

[CLICK the 🔔 bell icon in the top-right]
"They didn't wait for anyone to log in. Four things needed a human's eye
overnight, and they're queued right here."

[CLICK the top notification — Tax & Compliance Agent]
"That takes us straight to the responsible agent — the Tax & Compliance
Agent — and shows exactly what it did, when, and why it flagged for review."


────────────────────────────────────────
  ACT 2 — EACH AGENT IN PLAIN ENGLISH (2 minutes)
────────────────────────────────────────

[CLICK the ✕ in the drawer to close it, then scroll back up to the tile row]
"Let's look at the whole team of agents. Five core ones, organised the way
D365 already organises tiles — Revenue on the left, Operations in the
middle, Governance on the right."

[POINT at the tiles, reading one italic line aloud from each]
"Every agent introduces itself in plain English. 'I'm reconciling creator
royalties against contracts.' 'I'm chasing overdue invoices.' 'I'm
monitoring tax obligations.' No jargon, no ticket queues."

[CLICK the Royalty & Creator Agent tile]
"Here's what the Royalty agent did this morning. Twelve statements
reconciled by 09:42. Two discrepancies flagged on Creator #4471 — the rate
in the invoice didn't match contract version 2.1. It didn't fix it silently.
It flagged it and stopped."

[CLICK inside the "Ask this agent" input, TYPE: "Show me royalties for #4471"
 then press Enter]
"And when the controller wants more detail, they don't file a ticket. They
just ask. Natural language, in-line, right where the work is."

[CLICK ✕ to close the Royalty drawer]
[CLICK the Tax & Compliance Agent tile]
"Same pattern for every agent. Tax & Compliance shows three VAT returns
approaching the 30th of September deadline. Draft schedules for corporation
tax already prepared and in review."

[CLICK ✕ to close]


────────────────────────────────────────
  ACT 3 — END-TO-END, NOT JUST TASKS (90 seconds)
────────────────────────────────────────

[SCROLL down to "End-to-End Process Agents"]
"These next three are different. They're not point solutions — each one
owns a full end-to-end process. Look at the flow across the top of each
card."

[GESTURE — do NOT click — at the AP Exception flow chips]
"AP Exception Agent: Invoice Matching, then Exception Detection, then
Vendor Escalation, then Resolution Tracking. Two-hundred-and-forty-seven
invoices matched today. Ninety-four percent auto-resolved. Your AP team
only touches the eight real problems."

[CLICK anywhere on the AP Exception card body]
"That's what opening the workspace looks like from here."

[Return to the dashboard by clicking the browser back button OR just
 scroll — the toast confirms the intent]

[GESTURE at the Vendor Query and Close & Consolidation cards without
 clicking the flow steps]
"Same shape. Vendor Query Agent answers PO and payment questions from
vendors twenty-four seven — sixty-three queries today, five escalated.
Close & Consolidation Agent runs the month-end sequence — six of eight
entities closed, on track for Sep 30."


────────────────────────────────────────
  CLOSING (30 seconds)
────────────────────────────────────────

[SCROLL to the Business Assistance charts at the bottom]
"And this is the operating-model shift, right here."

[POINT at the line chart — do NOT click on it]
"Blue solid line: actions taken by agents — up and to the right. Purple
dashed line: manual actions taken by humans — declining. That crossover
IS the business case for REACH."

[POINT at the donut on the left]
"And the mix — where agents are spending their time — is now something
you can steer, not something buried in a ticket queue."

[Optional close]
"That's the demo. Every button on the top bar works — try the search icon,
the settings, the entity switcher. Every agent tile expands. Every alert
in the bell links to the underlying agent. Have a play."

  5. QUICK RECOVERY IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG DURING A DEMO
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Problem                              Fix
  ─────────────────────────────────    ─────────────────────────────────
  A dropdown won't close                Press Escape
  An agent drawer stuck open            Click the ✕, or refresh the page
  Layout looks squashed                 Widen the browser to 1400px+
  Logos don't load                      Check that reach-logo.svg and
                                        Cognizantlogo.png are both in the
                                        same folder as the .html
  Clicked something and nothing         Almost certainly a "DO NOT CLICK"
    happened                            element — see section 3 above
  Need to reset the demo state          Refresh the page (Ctrl/Cmd + R)

  7. KNOWN CAVEATS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  • All buttons are cosmetic — no real backend. Every intended click
    surfaces a toast so you know it registered.
  • Charts are static SVG for reliability during offline demos.
  • "Ask this agent" accepts any text but doesn't call an LLM — swap
    the onkeydown handler if wiring to a real endpoint.
  • Optimised for Chrome, Edge, and Safari at 1440px+ width.
